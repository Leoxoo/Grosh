# Split parts are independent real transactions

Splitting a transaction creates one ordinary transaction per extra part, sharing a link so they show as Related transactions, and the original keeps its identity and shows the remainder. Undo split merges the parts back into the original. Unlike the two halves of a transfer (ADR 0002), editing a part never offers to update the others: a part is a real transaction the user may later move to another day or category, and the total is free to change, so there is no invariant between the parts for an edit to protect. We considered a single record with a list of parts, but every balance, list, search and report already works on transactions, and parts would have needed their own paths through all of them. The trade-off is that the parts can drift apart from the original amount, which is intended; Undo split is the only way back to one transaction.
