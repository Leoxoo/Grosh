# Forgiving a Debt or Loan marks the original instead of adding transactions

Forgiving what is outstanding on a Loan or Debt adds no transactions. The original is marked forgiven with a date, a category and a note, and the forgiven amount counts in reports as income (Debt) or expense (Loan) on the forgiven date. This supersedes the forgiveness half of ADR 0003: paying still records linked transactions and the original keeps its amount and date, but forgiving no longer writes a Debt Collection or Repayment plus an offsetting income or expense. That pair showed the user money that never moved, three rows for one favour. Balances still reconcile to the bank, because no money moved and the original and the real payments are untouched. Forgiveness recorded under the old rule stays as it is.
