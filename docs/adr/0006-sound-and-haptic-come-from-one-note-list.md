# The Success cue's sound and haptic are generated from one note list

The Success cue's sound and its haptic both come from a single list of notes (pitch, start time, loudness, ring): a script kept in the repo renders the sound from it, and the app generates the haptic from the very same list at runtime. Each haptic tap lands on its note's start, its strength follows the note's loudness and its sharpness follows the note's pitch, and under a note that rings with sparkle, as Income's last note does, a soft buzz fades out as the note rings. That makes them match by construction. We rejected recorded audio paired with hand-authored haptic files: the Duolingo sounds that inspired the cue aren't ours to use, and a haptic tuned by ear to a recording drifts out of step the moment either is touched. The cost is that the sounds are synthesized, so simpler than a studio recording, and swapping in a recording later means authoring its haptic by hand.
