# Reimbursements are stored on the expense, not as transactions

Money someone pays back against an Expense is kept as an entry on that Expense (an amount, a day, an optional note), and the Expense's net, its amount plus its Reimbursements, lands in the wallet on the Expense's own day. This supersedes in part the reasoning of ADR 0003, which refused to rewrite the original so that balances keep reconciling against bank statements. We accept the cost knowingly: a wallet's balance can differ from its bank between the Expense's day and the day the money actually arrived, and entering a late Reimbursement changes past balances. In exchange, the Card statement keeps using the original amount, so the Card still reconciles with what it was charged, and a shared dinner shows as one row (`-$50  = -$20`) instead of a cluster of income transactions that would count as income in reports. A Reimbursement paid into a different wallet is not a Reimbursement; the user records it as a transfer or income.
